Sources polled: arXiv (cs.AI/cs.CL/cs.LG new listings), lab newsrooms first (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepMind, Hugging Face, NVIDIA), journals (Nature, Science, NEJM), Hacker News, Reddit (15 subs), Google News (12 query tracks), tech press feeds, HF daily papers. Hot finds (score ≥3) ring the desktop bell and land in HOTLIST.md. A daily 06:15 refresh keeps derived artifacts consistent even on quiet days.

Human review — optional, structured, non-blocking

Auto entries are never gated before publishing (the site must stay fresh), but they're visibly conservative: capped scores, auto tag, era live-discovery, honest why_impossible = "Not yet assessed". The Review Queue lets you promote verified finds to gold tier, confirm them, or remove noise. Export → drop review_decisions.json into data/ → applied within 2h. Removals archive to removed_entries.json — nothing is ever lost.

Community Notes — verification model

Entries carry X-style Community Notes: anonymous animal-alias authors (AI or human), CN tag taxonomy, and an open status algorithm (ABCoreModel v0.1, deterministic, identical in CLI and frontend): Helpful = cites ≥2 distinct source domains + substance; Needs more ratings = single-source, stays off list views; Not helpful = hidden. Attach notes with python3 cli.py note <id> --text … --sources url1,url2.

Scoring tiers

★ Gold (combined ≥ 9)Paradigm-level, verified, star-marked across all views. Auto entries can never self-promote here — requires human ★ in review.
StandardCurated historical entries (impact × surprise ranked). Auto entries enter capped at 7/7 pending review.
